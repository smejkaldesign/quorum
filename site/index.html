01

How it works

1

Capture

Quorum detects Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls and records them on your Mac. Transcription runs on the device.

2

Write

When a call ends, Quorum writes the notes as plain markdown into your Agent OS folder, next to the files you already keep.

3

Ask

Ask questions about any meeting through Claude Code or your own API key. Anthropic, OpenAI and local models through Ollama are supported.

Nothing leaves the Mac unless you turn sync on.
