How it works

  1. Connect

    Connect the tools you already use: Granola for meeting notes, your calendar, and the folder where you keep your notes. Quorum imports what they write. For notes of your own, start listening in Quorum or dictate.

  2. Write

    Quorum files each note as plain markdown in your local memory, next to your other agent memories, and updates related notes with what changed. You choose which notes your team sees.

  3. Ask

    Ask questions across everything Quorum knows, in Quorum or from the AI tools you already use through the Quorum MCP server or CLI. Bring your own keys to stay in control of costs.
