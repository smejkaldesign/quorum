Quorum

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

Summary

Quorum is a macOS app that transcribes meetings and writes notes into markdown files you own. In the free Local edition, everything stays on your Mac. In Quorum Cloud, we store your notes for sync and route call audio through our relay to a speech provider. We do not store call audio. We do not sell your data. We do not use your content to train models, and neither do our providers: Anthropic does not train on API inputs or outputs, every Deepgram request carries the model-training opt-out, and our AssemblyAI account is opted out of model training, which also gives zero retention of streaming audio and transcripts.

This policy is published by Smejkal Design, a sole proprietorship based in Portland, Oregon, United States. Contact: eric@smejkal.design.

The Local edition

Audio, transcripts and notes stay on your Mac. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you take one of these two actions.

  • You turn sync on. Sync requires a Quorum Cloud account and is covered below.
  • You choose a cloud speech or model provider and enter your own key. Your audio or text then goes to that provider under that provider's terms and privacy policy. We never see it.

Smejkal Design receives no data from the Local edition with sync off and no cloud provider set.

Quorum Cloud: sync

With sync on, we store your notes (path, frontmatter and body), links between notes, meeting records (title, start and end times, participants, visibility), transcript segments (text, speaker and timestamps), chat messages, and attachments you add on our servers for your workspace. Our database is hosted by Supabase in the United States.

  • Private notes are visible only to their owner.
  • Notes you mark public become visible to other workspace members after a workspace admin includes them.

Quorum Cloud: managed transcription

With managed transcription, call audio streams from your Mac through our relay to a speech provider, either AssemblyAI or Deepgram. The provider returns text. We do not store the audio. The relay logs session starts, durations, engine names, workspace identifiers and error messages. It keeps no audio or transcript text. Relay logs are deleted within 30 days.

The running summary and notes may be generated by a model provider, Anthropic, using our API keys. Transcript text is sent to Anthropic for that purpose. Each provider processes that data only to return a result to us: Anthropic does not train on API inputs or outputs, Deepgram requests carry the model-training opt-out, and our AssemblyAI account is opted out of model training with zero retention for streaming.

Sign-in and account data

You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email address through Resend, our email provider. We store your email address and your workspace membership and role. We do not store passwords.

Billing data

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects and stores your card details under its own privacy policy. We store only Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. We never see or store card numbers.

Usage metering

To bill transcription hours, we store the number of managed transcription hours used per seat per month. This record does not include audio or transcript content.

Service providers

We share data with these providers only to run the service.

  • Supabase: database and sync storage, United States.
  • AssemblyAI and Deepgram: speech to text for managed transcription.
  • Anthropic: summaries and notes generation.
  • Stripe: payments and subscription billing.
  • Resend: sign-in code emails.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

Recording consent

You must have the consent of meeting participants where recording consent laws require it. You are responsible for that compliance. Quorum cannot know who is in your meetings or where they are, so that responsibility stays with you. Quorum includes a STOP word. When a participant says it, the recording stops.

Where data is stored

Quorum Cloud data is stored in the United States. If you use Quorum from outside the United States, your data is transferred to and processed there.

Retention and deletion

We keep your Cloud notes and account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and workspace data by emailing eric@smejkal.design. We delete the data within 30 days, except for records that billing and tax law require us to keep. Notes on your Mac are files you control, and deleting your account does not remove them.

Your choices

  • Use the Local edition with sync off and no cloud provider. No data reaches us.
  • Turn sync or managed transcription off at any time in the app.
  • Mark notes private so only you can see them.
  • Email us to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you.

Quorum Cloud is offered to users in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR or the CCPA. Email us and we will honor access, correction, portability and deletion requests from anyone, wherever you are.

Security

Data moves between the app, the relay and our database over TLS. Our database provider encrypts stored data at rest with AES-256. Only the owner of Smejkal Design has access to production systems, and secrets are kept in provider secret stores, never in the app. No system is fully secure, and we cannot promise that unauthorized access will never happen. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Children

Quorum is not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We post the new version on this site and email workspace admins. The effective date at the top shows the current version.
